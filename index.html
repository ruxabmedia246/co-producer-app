Co-Producer

for iPhone and iPad

Co-Producer opens the board of your own Co-Producer computer, the system a TV show's producer uses to book guests, build the running order and follow the show. It is made for the producer and production team of one show.

Getting started

On the first screen, enter your board's address. Setup Check on your Co-Producer computer shows it, or that computer can give you a link that fills it in. Then sign in with the board's own password; after that, Face ID opens it.

No Co-Producer computer? Tap Try the demo to see every screen with made-up shows and guests.

Common questions

The app says the board is not answering. Your device has to reach your Co-Producer computer: on the same network, or through the private network your Co-Producer uses. The app can show the last board it saw meanwhile.

Alerts or the Live Activity don't appear. Allow notifications for Co-Producer in Settings, then open the app once so it can tell your Co-Producer where to send them.
