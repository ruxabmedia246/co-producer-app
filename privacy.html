Co-Producer support · Privacy

Privacy

Co-Producer for iPhone and iPad · last updated 16 October 2026

Co-Producer opens the board of a Co-Producer computer run by you or your show. There is no Co-Producer service behind the app: it sends nothing to the app's developer as a service provider, and nothing to anyone else.

What the app connects to

Only the board address you enter (your own Co-Producer computer, usually on your own private network). What you see in the app, such as bookings, running orders and messages, comes from that computer and stays between it and your device.

What is kept on your device

The board's address and your sign-in to it, kept in your device's keychain, on this device only. Signing out in the app removes the sign-in.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your device gives the app a notification token, which the app sends to your own Co-Producer computer so it can send you alerts through Apple's push notification service. The token is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Sharing files

When you share a photo or file to Co-Producer from another app, it goes to your own Co-Producer computer and nowhere else.

The demo

The demo shows made-up shows and guests that are built into the app. Nothing in the demo is sent anywhere.

No tracking, no analytics, no advertising

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and shares nothing with third parties.
